About

I am an MSc Physics student at University College London, working at the intersection of radar altimetry, sea-ice remote sensing, and deep learning. My current research focuses on generating and classifying radar altimetry echoes from satellite observations over Arctic sea ice.

My broader interests include physics-aware deep learning, satellite image processing, scientific machine learning, and data-driven methods for geophysical and astronomical observations.
