About Me

I am an MSci Physics graduate from University College London, working at the intersection of radar altimetry, sea-ice remote sensing, and deep learning. Working with Dr. Michel Tsamados at the Centre for Polar Observation and Modelling (CPOM), I apply AI-driven methods to cryospheric remote sensing.

My broader interests include physics-informed deep learning, scientific machine learning, satellite image processing, and data-driven methods for geophysical and astronomical observations. I am particularly interested in extending these approaches to InSAR-based surface-deformation monitoring, glacier and permafrost change detection, and satellite-driven natural-hazard assessment.

Ongoing Research

DL4Landslide: Landslide Displacement and Thickness Inversion

Developing deep-learning methods to map landslide surface displacement from multi-temporal elevation data. Combining surface motion, elevation change, and mass conservation to estimate landslide thickness and displaced volume for natural-hazard assessment.

arXiv preprint · Co-author

LATED: Searching for Metal-free and Extremely Metal-poor Star Formation

Lyα-anchored photometric selection of candidate metal-free and extremely metal-poor star-forming galaxies, using JWST and Roman filter diagnostics to prioritise targets for spectroscopic follow-up.

Read paper

Deep Learning for Radar Altimetry Echo Generation in Sea Ice Surface Image Recognition

Generating physically consistent SAR altimetry waveforms from Sentinel-3 OLCI sea-ice surface imagery using deep generative models.
